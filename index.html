Sachkhand Kirtan Privacy Policy

App: Sachkhand Kirtan (Android package com.pvskhalsa.app.sachkhandkirtan)
Developer: PVS Khalsa
Last updated: November 25, 2026

Sachkhand Kirtan plays live kirtan and on-demand Hukamnama, katha and Sangrand audio. This policy covers versions that may remain installed as well as the current release. Features differ by app version.

Accounts and information

The app has no accounts or sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The app does not upload your favorites, search history, settings, recordings or voice-search text to the developer. The developer does not sell this information. If you choose to email the developer, your email provider delivers your message and contact information to that address. External services receive the requests needed for features you use, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, search history, settings, playback choices and a small record of review-request timing are stored in private app storage. The app disables Android automatic app backup; device-maker transfer tools may behave differently.

When you record a live channel, the app saves the channel audio as an MP3 in private app storage. It does not record the microphone or upload recordings. You can delete a recording in the app or save a copy with Android's file picker. Copies saved elsewhere remain there until you delete them separately.

If you open a local audio or video file from another app, Sachkhand Kirtan makes a temporary private copy to play it. It does not upload that file.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data. Save any recordings you want to keep first.

In versions with Home screen shortcuts, a shortcut is added only after you request it and the launcher accepts or confirms the request. The launcher stores the channel's shortcut label, icon and app-opening information. Launcher behavior is controlled by Android and your launcher. A shortcut request does not guarantee a shortcut was added. In versions with the system shortcut option (Android 11 and later), you can choose one channel for an Android accessibility shortcut. The app stores that chosen channel in private app storage. You assign the shortcut yourself in Android settings, and pressing it plays the chosen channel. The app does not use an Android Accessibility service, does not read your screen and does not observe your key presses. You can remove the choice in the app.

Network requests and other services

Playback connects to the selected station's stream provider. Playlist updates and the opening audio use GitHub; on-demand audio uses SGPC. While a live station plays, the app also asks that station's server about once a minute for the current title and listener count. Those providers can receive your IP address and request information under their own policies. Streams and some supplied links may use HTTP rather than HTTPS; the app does not promise that every stream is encrypted.

Older versions also retrieve Blogger announcements, including scheduled background checks. Version 26.11.2 is designed to remove the Blogger feed and retire its scheduled work when it is installed and opened. This description does not mean that the update is already available on your testing track or device. Users who still run an older version may continue making those requests until they update or stop using that version.

When you choose a website, social/group link, contact link or map from the app, the selected app or website handles the request under its own policy. For a channel's Location action, the app asks before opening Maps and sends a station name or place search, not your device's GPS location. Sachkhand Kirtan does not request location permission or read GPS for this feature.

Google Play features

Google Play In-App Updates checks for available app updates through the Play Store. Google's documentation describes processing of device metadata, app version and installed module/asset-pack information to check update availability and size. The developer does not receive these update-check details through the app.

Versions with Google Play In-App Review can ask Google Play to show its rating/review flow after app use. You choose whether to submit a rating or review. Google Play handles that submission. Google's documentation says submitted rating and free-text review data is encrypted and used for a review on the Play Store; feedback may instead be shared privately with the developer under Google Play's feedback rules. You can delete a submitted review through your Play Store or Google Account. Calling the API does not guarantee the review dialog appears. The app does not collect your Google password or receive the text you submit through this API.

Sources: https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates and https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review

Voice search

If you choose voice search, Android's separate speech recognition service, such as a service from Google or your device maker, handles your speech under its own policy and permission settings. The app receives the resulting search text. Sachkhand Kirtan itself does not request microphone permission.

Permissions

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE support streaming, playlist retrieval and connectivity. Older versions also use them for announcements.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK and WAKE_LOCK support playback while the app is not visible or the screen is off.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS supports media controls; older versions may also show announcements.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED may remain declared by the scheduling library. Older versions use scheduled work for announcement checks; version 26.11.2 removes those announcement checks. A declared permission is not a claim that a feature collects personal data.

No camera, contacts, device-location or microphone permission is requested by the app.

Content from third-party sources and removal requests

The app lists and plays content from third-party websites and services, including live kirtan streams, SGPC audio and the GitHub channel playlist. Older versions also show Blogger announcements. Rights in this content remain with their respective owners.

If you would like any content removed from the app, email the support address shown on the app's Google Play listing, naming the content and giving your reason, and the developer will remove it.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when app behavior changes. The date above will be updated when a changed policy is published. For questions, use the developer contact option on the app's Google Play listing.
