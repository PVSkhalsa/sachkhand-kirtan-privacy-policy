Sachkhand Kirtan Privacy Policy

App: Sachkhand Kirtan (Android package com.pvskhalsa.app.sachkhandkirtan)
Developer: PVS Khalsa

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Sachkhand Kirtan is an audio player for live kirtan, Hukamnama and katha audio. This policy describes what the app does with information.

What we collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. The developer does not receive or store your favorites, settings, recordings or voice-search results. The app does not sell this information. External services used by the app may process request information as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Favorites, settings and playback choices are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The app turns Android's automatic app backup off, so the app does not opt in to it.
  • If you tap Record while listening to a live channel, the app saves a copy of that live stream as an MP3 in the app's private storage. The app does not upload them. You can save a copy using Android's file picker, and you can delete any recording from the Recordings screen.
  • If you open an audio or video file from another app, the app makes a temporary copy in its private storage to play it. The app does not send it anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes this private app data. Copies you save outside the app remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately. Save copies of recordings first if you want to keep them. Device-maker transfer tools may behave differently from Android automatic backup.

Network use

To play audio and show announcements the app connects to the internet and requests files from these services: GitHub (live stream playlist), SGPC (Hukamnama and katha audio) and Blogger (announcements feed). Like any website, these services can see your IP address and device request details under their own privacy policies. The app does not send them your name, account or contact data.
When the app is installed from Google Play, it uses Google's Play In-App Updates library. The Play Store processes device metadata, the app version, and a list of installed modules and asset packs to check update availability and size. Google's documentation says this data is encrypted, not transferred to third parties, and deleted after a fixed retention period. The developer does not receive this information. Source: https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates (last updated January 30, 2026).

Voice search

If you tap voice search, Android's speech recognition service on your device (provided by Google or your phone maker) handles your speech under its own policy. The app only receives the text result.

Permissions and why

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: stream audio, retrieve playlists and announcements, and check connectivity.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: keep audio playback running while the app is not visible.
  • WAKE_LOCK: keep playback running when the screen is off.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show media controls and announcements.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: let scheduled announcement checks restart after your phone reboots.

The app does not request microphone access. Its recording feature saves the playing audio stream, not sound from your microphone. Voice search is handled by the separate Android speech recognition service, which controls its own permissions and processing.

Content from third-party sources and removal requests

The app plays and lists audio and announcements retrieved from third-party websites and services, including live kirtan streams and Hukamnama and katha audio from SGPC (hs.sgpc.net), announcements from a Blogger feed, and a channel playlist hosted on GitHub. Rights in this content remain with their respective owners.

If you would like any content removed from the app, send an email to the contact email address shown on the app's Google Play listing, naming the content and giving your reason, and the developer will remove it.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
